The idea

A bridge you can stand on.

Foray
A venture into new ground. For a global company, that ground is India.
Bridge
What carries a company across, from a mandate at headquarters to a centre that runs.
The arch
The strongest shape a bridge can take. Load makes it firmer, not weaker. Accountability, built.

How it is built

Two pillars and a span.

The F stands upright. The B leans in and carries the load across. Read the initials as a drawing and they become a bridge, cut from a classical Caslon letter so it reads as established, not invented yesterday.

PillarsEqual weight, upright and grounded.
SpanOne unbroken curve, from mandate to opening day.

The Arch

The arch holds things.

One hero arch per page, always upright, always standing on the ground of the layout. Never a decoration on its own.

The Seal

Every commitment, sealed.

The seal closes what ForayBridge promises: a signed proposal, a partner’s letter, a welcome kit for a new centre. It appears once, at the end, the way a signature does.
